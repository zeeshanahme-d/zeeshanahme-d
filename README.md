## ⭐ Featured project

### [Eyecatch AI](https://eyecatch.zeeshanahmed.dev): AI thumbnail generator

Describe your video, pick a platform, style and colors, and Google Gemini generates the thumbnail. I built the frontend **and** the entire backend.

- **Credits that can't be double-spent:** deducted in one atomic MongoDB update before the AI call; failed or stuck generations are refunded automatically
- **Solid auth:** JWT in httpOnly cookies, rotating refresh tokens with reuse detection, and sign-out on every device after a password change
- **Abuse protection:** rate limits per IP, email and user, hashed email codes that expire in 10 minutes, disposable-email blocking
- **Billing:** Paddle with signature-verified webhooks (kept in test mode on purpose)
- **Background jobs:** monthly credit refills, recycle-bin cleanup and trending scores
- **Community gallery:** likes, a trending sort, and share links with social previews

`React 19` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Tailwind CSS` `TanStack Query` `Zustand` `Node.js` `Express 5` `MongoDB` `Zod` `Gemini` `Cloudinary` `Paddle`
<br />Client on Vercel, API on Oracle Cloud behind Nginx.

🌐 **Live:** [eyecatch.zeeshanahmed.dev](https://eyecatch.zeeshanahmed.dev) &nbsp;·&nbsp; 💻 **Code:** [zeeshanahme-d/Ai-thumbnail-generator](https://github.com/zeeshanahme-d/Ai-thumbnail-generator)
